A spider follows the cursor.

Move your mouse. A spider will crawl in and find it.

Your pointer stays your pointer. The spider is its own animal: move around and it crawls after you, slowly, swinging round in an arc when it has to turn, and stops a little short while you are moving. Hold still and it walks the last of the way in and settles on the tip, and while it sits there the arrow is hidden. Move, and the arrow is yours again. Click, hover and type as usual.

Put it on your page

One line. Nothing to host, no build step:

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/spider-crawl-cursor@1/spider-cursor.min.js"></script>

Options go on the tag: data-size="40" data-speed="1.5" data-perch="false" data-skin="widow". Or npm i spider-crawl-cursor.
